# Run AI models on your own PC

The new Models page shows which local models your PC can run. You can also see how much of your cloud use a local model could take.

## Pick, download and test a local model

Open Settings, then Models. Each model shows a fit label for your PC. You can download a model, load it, test its speed and delete it. Press Compare models to run one prompt on two models and pick the better answer before the names show.

If Ollama is off, press Start Ollama. If the Ollama (local) account is off, press Turn on so your models reach the chat picker.

## See what a local model could save you

The Token report on the Models page counts your recent cloud replies. It shows how many a local model could answer. It reads counts only, never your messages, and stays on your computer. Cached input shows apart, so the totals are not overstated.
