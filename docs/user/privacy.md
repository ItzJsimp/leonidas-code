# Privacy: usage numbers

Leonidas Code can send anonymous usage numbers. It does this only if you click **Yes, share usage**. If you click **No thanks**, the app sends nothing.

## What we send

- The app version and your system type (Windows, macOS or Linux).
- How often you open the app and how long you use it.
- Which areas and main buttons you open.
- Which setup step you reached and how long setup took.
- How many skills and task cards your AIOS folder has. It also tells us if a memory folder exists.
- Which AI services you connect, from a fixed list.
- How many messages you send.
- A short type label when an error screen, an unexpected close, an update or a feedback send happens.

## Only when you ask for it

- **I'm stuck.** When you press **I'm stuck** or say "Leonidas, I'm stuck", the app shows you a short report first. It holds the screen name, your last action, an error type, the AI service name, the app version and your system type. It has a random report ID that is not your install ID. You choose **Send report** or **Cancel**. We keep the report ID so the app can tell you when the problem is fixed. The app asks our service about its own report IDs and nothing else.
- **Friends leaderboard.** It is off until you turn it on in Settings, General, Privacy. Then the app sends your weekly counts (words dictated, messages sent, days you dictated), a random ID made only for the leaderboard and the invite code. Friends see you as "Friend 1", "Friend 2" and so on. They see your nickname only if you turn on **Show my nickname**. Turning the leaderboard off removes your counts from our service.

## What we never send

- Your messages, replies or code.
- File names, folder paths or card titles.
- Keys, passwords or email addresses.
- Your IP address. The service discards it.

## Who you are to us

The app makes a random ID on your computer. It has no name, email or account. Testers can add an optional tester name. Nobody else can add a name.

## Where the numbers go and how long we keep them

The numbers go to PostHog Cloud in the EU. The retention period is the PostHog setting for our project. We will state the exact period here when we set it.

## How to stop

Open Settings, General, Privacy. Turn off **Share anonymous usage**. The app stops at once.

## How to delete your numbers

Open Settings, General, Privacy and copy your install ID. Send it to us with **Send feedback** and ask us to delete it. We delete all numbers with that ID.

## Changes

If we add a new kind of number, the app asks you again before it sends it.
