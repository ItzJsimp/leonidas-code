# Executive Digest: Top Preprints for Jake (Oct 16, 2026 Sweep)

## Most Promising Frameworks

1. **Z-SPACE: A Multi-Agent Tool Orchestration Framework for Enterprise-Grade LLM Automation** (Jing Nan, 2025, SSRN 5896270) – Provides a scalable orchestration layer for coordinating multiple LLM tools, addressing trust, delegation, and cascading failure mitigation in production environments.

2. **PAKD: Resource-Adaptive In-Network Inference with a Distilled Heterogeneous Model Pool** (jun huang, Mai Zhang, Taiwu Yao, 2026, SSRN 7571278) – Optimizes local inference by dynamically selecting and adapting heterogeneous distilled models, reducing latency and compute costs for edge deployments.

3. **Positive Safety Constraints: A Learned-Concept Architecture for AI Guardrails** (Frederick Roth, 2026, SSRN 7119040) – Introduces a learned-concept-based guardrail architecture that enforces safety constraints at the system level, with strong empirical validation on alignment sandbox datasets.

4. **Multi-Agent Orchestration: Coordination, Trust, and Cascading Failures** (Rizwan Tanveer, 2026, SSRN 6734798) – Analyzes failure propagation patterns in multi-agent systems and proposes resilience mechanisms for reliable enterprise automation.

5. **A Long-Term Construction Memory System for Infrastructure Digital Twins** (Dongwook Kim, 2026, SSRN 6947979) – Proposes a six-layer cognitive memory architecture for LLM-augmented digital twin reasoning, relevant for infrastructure monitoring and decision support.

## Key Takeaways

- **Multi-Agent Orchestration** (Z-SPACE, Multi-Agent Orchestration) is the highest-impact area for Jake's automation goals, offering concrete patterns for tool delegation and failure isolation.
- **Local Efficiency** (PAKD) directly reduces compute costs, aligning with Jake's focus on lean, cost-effective architectures.
- **Safety Guardrails** (Positive Safety Constraints) provide a principled approach to embedding alignment constraints into production systems.
- All five papers are openly accessible via SSRN and offer immediate applicability to current research and deployment pipelines.
